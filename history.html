2023 to now

The Story So Far

A timeline of the club, pulled from the forum thread where it all started. Click Post to read the original.

2023

  • Jul 31: Cody (Pun1sh3R) starts the club. First reply: “I guess sign me up or whatever.” Post
  • Sep 1: The charter-member window closes. Post
  • Oct 9: First look at the Workhorse CAD. Post
  • Oct 20: The first club meeting, on Zoom. Post
  • Nov 2: A motion: make a club shirt and give the proceeds to Toys for Tots. “Any seconds on this Motion?” Post
  • Nov 17: The Throw-Yo holiday fundraiser ends with $130 raised. Post
  • Dec 3: The club's first donation to the YoYoExpert drive: $70, or 28 yo-yos. Four days later it was 32. Post
  • Dec 18: Still waiting on eight Workhorse prototypes. One is set aside for a Pay It Forward. Post

2024

  • Jan 22: Monthly meetings are for all members. Camera optional. Voice or chat is fine. Post
  • Jan 24: The charter is adopted.
  • Jan 30: The members-only Participation Trophy event is announced, with a Respawn and a Workhorse prototype as prizes. Post
  • Feb 3: Errant.Giraffe posts the Modern Responsive March logo. Post
  • Mar 11: The Official Yoyo Boomer Document arrives, with original art by Higby. Post
  • Mar 25: Workhorse V2 prototype pre-order opens to members. Post
  • Apr 3: Club meeting recap: the club will get more selective with sponsors, quality over quantity. Post
  • Apr 29: Unresponsive prototype pre-order opens: 20 units, $100 each, $20 per order to the club fund. Post
  • Aug 16: The club becomes a Bronze sponsor of the Ante Yoyo Cup, paid for with merch and prototype money. Post
  • Sep 23: Workhorse production plan: founding members, members and public colorways. Anything over cost goes back to the club. Post
  • Nov 29: The 2024 Toys for Tots drive merch drops. Post
  • Dec 28: Planning Fixed Axle February 2025 starts, and members are asked for themes. Post

2025

  • Jan 13: Word that the Workhorse won't be ready for Modern Responsive March. Post
  • Apr 17: The production Workhorse lands. Post
  • Jul 3: The Operator prototype V2 pre-order opens: 9 units minimum, 18 max, $110 each. Post
  • Oct 20: A Workhorse Lite is floated, which would need 40 pre-orders. New members join the list. Post
