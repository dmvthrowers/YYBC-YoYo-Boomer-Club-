Sponsor the club

Every prize in a drawing was a company saying yes to a very silly idea.

  • Event Donor (a product under $100): a mention in the thread and the event video credits.
  • Event Sponsor ($100 or more in product or funds): your logo on the event video banners, a thread and social post, and a Linktree link for the event. Ideally you ship prizes straight to the winners.
  • Club Sponsor (an annual commitment of $500 or more): your logo on every banner all year, a year-round Linktree spot, and social posts.
