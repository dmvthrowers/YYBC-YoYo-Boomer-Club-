Modern Responsive March 2026

The wasteland is quiet. The dust has settled. A month-long, nine-trick, old-school ladder with a Fallout theme and a lot of atomic puns.

The setup

  • Same relaxed month-long format as Fixed Axle February 2026.
  • Theme: Fallout. The thread greeted everyone with "Welcome, fellow dwellers."
  • The trick list was called Radioactive: old-school tricks with names to match.
  • Prizes came from ZGRT, Sickburn Yo-Yo Workshop / Return Bottoms, and Throw-Yo.
  • A member built a mobile-friendly trick reference app for the ladder.

The trick list: Radioactive

  1. Pop the Clutch
  2. Stop and Go
  3. Ripcord
  4. Zipper (2-3 times)
  5. Rewind
  6. Split the Atom (2-3 rotations)
  7. Atomic Bomb (2-3 times)
  8. Hydrogen Bomb
  9. Cold Fusion (responsive)
