Claim the Title

What's a Yoyo Boomer?

"Sure, you can DNA… but can you Rock the Baby?"

On July 31, 2023, Cody (Pun1sh3R) posted on the YoYoExpert forums that he was starting an online club for anyone willing to claim the title "Yoyo Boomer." First reply, 17 minutes later: "I guess sign me up or whatever." By August 2 there were 46 charter members. That's the whole origin story.

You might be a Boomer if:

  • You still throw high-wall organics on purpose.
  • You have a take on every yo-yo you've ever touched.
  • You own more yo-yos than tricks you can land clean.
  • You want the kids off your lawn, right after you teach them Walk the Dog.
  • You tell dad jokes between binds.

Club Business

Every club needs a committee. Ours is mostly jokes.

  • Pay It Forward. Mail a yo-yo to the next Boomer on the list.
  • Toys for Tots with Throw-Yo and YoYoExpert. $130 in 2023. $1,300 in 2024.
  • The Workhorse. Our club yo-yo with G Squared, prototype to production. More in the workshop.
  • Art. Turtles, bombs and lawn mowers from the idea folder.
  • Pins and holo stickers. Every cent goes back into the club.
  • Meetings. About once a month, on Zoom. Somebody makes a motion. Somebody seconds it. It's all in the charter, and the whole story is in the timeline.
