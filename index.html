Who We Are

YYBC is a club for folks who lived through a yo-yo boom. Maybe you threw every day. Maybe you just watched. Either way, you belong here.

We pick the yo-yo back up. We share stories from back then. We learn what's changed since. All ages, all levels, always free.

The Booms

The yo-yo doesn't fade. It comes back in waves. Which one was yours?

1920s–1930s

The First Craze

Yo-yos hit American stores in the late 1920s. Contests and touring demonstrators spread the craze from town to town.

1960s

The TV Boom

Television ads and school demos put a yo-yo in millions of kids' pockets. Walk the Dog and Around the World went mainstream.

Late 1990s

The Bearing Boom

Ball-bearing axles made long spins easy. Tricks got bigger. Every lunch table had a thrower.

Today

Your Comeback

Modern yo-yos are unresponsive, smooth and wild. Bring your old one anyway. We'll show you what's new.

What We Do

  • Throw together and relearn the classics.
  • Swap stories, old yo-yos and boom-era memories.
  • Try modern yo-yos and new tricks at your own pace.
  • Pass the hobby on to kids and grandkids.
