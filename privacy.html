Privacy

This site doesn't collect anything about you. Here is the short version, and the details.

What we collect

  • Nothing. The site has no forms, no accounts, no comments and no sign-ups.
  • No cookies, no analytics and no ad trackers. The site runs no scripts at all.
  • Fonts and images load from this site, not from a third party.

Who else sees your visit

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep ordinary server logs of visits under its own privacy policy. Links on this site go to other places like YouTube, Instagram, YoYoExpert, Linktree and Throw-Yo. Once you click through, their policies apply, not ours.

If you email us

If you email yoyoboomerclub@gmail.com, we read it and answer it. We don't sell or share your email address.
