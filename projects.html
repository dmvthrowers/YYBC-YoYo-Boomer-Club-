The Operator

  • Mar 19, 2024: Cody asks the club what it wants in a Boomer unresponsive. Post. An early name idea back in November 2023 was The Flux Capacitor.
  • Apr 29 to May 2, 2024: prototype pre-order for members. 55.7 mm across, 43 mm wide, 63.5 g, G20 pads, diamond blasted, with a Boss Ripper bearing. 20 units max, $100 each shipped in the US, $20 of each order to the club fund. Post
  • Oct 1, 2024: G Squared live-tests the unresponsive prototypes. Post
  • Apr 15, 2025: Operator V2 goes back to the drawing board. The catch zone opens up a little and some weight moves to the rim. Post
  • Jul 3, 2025: V2 pre-order opens. 9 units minimum, 18 max, $110 each, $60 down and $50 on shipping. About a four month lead time. Post

Dead 'Orse

  • In Australia, ketchup is “dead 'orse.” Spinworthy's Glen offered the club a handmade wooden yo-yo with no bearing, and the name stuck. March 2024. Post
  • Jul 23, 2024: YYBC Dead 'Orses go up for sale. Post
  • Aug 2024: a second batch in golden wattle, Tasmanian blackwood and maple. A golden wattle one weighed 60 g and was $100 shipped to the US. Post
  • A prototype Dead 'Orse went around as a Pay It Forward through 2025.

Workhorse Lite

  • Jun 2025: Cody says the club will probably do a Workhorse Lite. Post

How a Prototype Gets Funded

  • Members pay for the experiment. Half goes down to place the order, the other half is due when the yo-yos are ready.
  • The people paying get a private group message to make decisions, like the prototype's color, and to give feedback.
  • In Cody's words: all prototypes with YYBC have been completed this way. “Self funded if you will.” Post
  • A little from each unit goes into the club fund. That money helped pay for sponsoring AYYC.

Pay It Forward

  • A Pay It Forward is a box of yo-yos that travels from member to member. You play it for a few weeks, then ship it to the next person who asks.
  • Dec 18, 2023: the first Workhorse prototype PIF, with weight rings, a bearing blank and a slimmer bearing, so the first people to try it could send feedback. Post
  • Nov 29, 2024: three Boomer PIFs were active: Workhorse V1, Workhorse V2 and a Dead 'Orse prototype. Volunteer in the thread when the one you want comes up. Post
