G Squared × YYBC

The Workhorse

A yo-yo built by the club with G Squared. One yo-yo that plays both 0A and 1A, with weights you can tune. Prototype to production, and yes, there's a Space Wagon.

The Yo-Yo

What it is

G Squared's design team wanted one yo-yo that plays two styles well and shows what "modern responsive" can mean. You can run it on a 5 mm or 4 mm bearing, or on a bearing blank for the Post Modern Responsive setup, and add 1 to 10 grams of weight rings to tune it.

The redistributed weight made the biggest difference between V1 and V2. Same size, different feel.

Specs

  • Style: 0A and 1A
  • Diameter: 55 mm
  • Width: 31.85 mm
  • Weight: 53.3 g
  • Material: 6061 aluminum
  • Bearing: Slim D, or a bearing blank (5 mm and 4 mm also supported)
  • Response: G2 Respawn pads

Measured specs from the production listing.

From prototype to production

  • Oct 9, 2023: First look at the CAD: 55 x 32 mm, a 3 mm gap and 53 to 54 grams, with cutouts that accept weight rings.
  • Oct 2023: Responsive prototype sent off for fabrication.
  • Dec 2023: V1 prototypes land at G Squared HQ.
  • Jan 22, 2024: Prototypes released into the wild for testing.
  • Mar 25, 2024: V2 prototype pre-order opens to members: $85, 20 units max, $20 per unit to the club fund.
  • Apr 2024: Unresponsive prototype ordered.
  • Jul 2024: V2 prototype arrives.
  • Aug 9, 2024: Club update: V2 is good to go toward production, with a member pre-order so every member can get one.
  • Nov 2024: Production order placed.
  • Apr 2025: Production release.
