The Charter

“Where Gravity Takes a Break, and So Do We Occasionally.” Eight articles, one lawn.

The short version

  • Who: all ages, any yo-yo, anyone who shares the club's values.
  • Meetings: around once a month, online, for ease of access.
  • Activities: workshops, themed challenges, casual talk about yo-yo techniques, and bourbon.
  • Culture: open-minded, funny, helpful and courteous. Set a good example.
  • Decisions: by consensus on the YYE forum or at the monthly meets. Everyone's input is welcome.

The Good Clauses

Article II, Section 3

“Get Off My Lawn”

Members are allowed to throw in a “get off my lawn” remark when necessary to add to the “Boomer” spirit.

Article II, Section 3.5

“…and Don’t Come Back”

Leave any time with no burned bridges. The YYBC will welcome you back with open arms.

Article VI

Amendments

The charter may be amended on a whim or when deemed necessary. Most importantly, have fun, love your throws, and your people.

Article VII, Section 3

Splitting the Atom

If the Arm Chair Quarterback / Camp Counselor(s) ever change, the club picks a volunteering exemplary or founding member to pass the “Yoyo Club Baton.” We are forever!
