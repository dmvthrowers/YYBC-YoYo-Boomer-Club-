Questions

FAQ

Short answers to the things people ask before they claim the title.

The short answers

Do I have to be old to be a Yoyo Boomer?

No. Boomer is a state of mind, not an age. Claim the title and you're a Boomer.

What does it cost?

Nothing. No dues, no age check, no tryouts.

How do I join?

Post in the club thread on the YoYoExpert forums and say you're in. We'll say welcome to the club.

Where does the club meet?

Online. About once a month there’s a club meeting on Zoom. Camera is optional, and you can talk or type in the chat. The club thread on the YoYoExpert forums is home base. We also use Discord for chat, YouTube for event videos and Instagram for photos. The Linktree has all the links.

What is Yovember?

Our November fundraiser. It starts November 1: grow a stash, talk yo-yo, throw tricks and support men's health.

Can I do the old challenges?

Yes. The events archive has the trick lists for every challenge we've run. Do them whenever you like and post in the thread.

What does “Peace, Love, and Chicken Grease” mean?

It’s how Matt Mullins signed off every video he hosted for Fixed Axle February and Modern Responsive March. We stole it for the footer.

Where does the money go?

Our holiday fundraiser supports YoYoExpert's Toys for Tots drive, and Yovember supports men's health. Money from club pins and stickers goes back into the club.

Is there a club yo-yo?

Yes, the Workhorse, built with G Squared Yoyos. It plays both 0A and 1A.

How do I sponsor the club?

Email yoyoboomerclub@gmail.com. The sponsors page has the options.

Is this the same as DMV Throwers?

No. DMV Throwers is a separate, free, in-person yo-yo club in DC, Maryland and Virginia. Some Boomers are also Throwers.
